Beyond the Bungalow

The objective

Reframe Tahiti from a passive luxury honeymoon backdrop into an active adventure and culture destination.

5-video campaign moves a viewer from “wow” to “wait, I could actually do that” to “saved, let's plan it”, while sending demand toward small local operators rather than large hospitality chains.

The role each one plays
01 Aspirational hook

Reef Shark Drift

Cinematic single shot · 5–20 sec · drone

One unbelievable image that buys attention and repositions the destination as wild, not just pretty. This is the shot that stops the scroll.

02 Share driver

You + Me… Moorea?

Shareable cut · 10–15 sec · couple-focused, text overlay

Built to be sent in a DM. It seeds the trip as a two-person decision and moves Moorea from feed to a conversation.

03 Cultural depth

Va'a Fishing

Unique experience · voiceover · 45–60 sec

Fishing the way Polynesians did 1,000 years ago, with the local guide who runs it as his business. Cultural depth and direct small-business support.

04 Adventure credibility

The Boat to Teahupo'o

Unique experience · voiceover · 45–60 sec

A globally famous wave most travelers assume is off-limits, shown as an accessible day trip with a local captain. Discovery, with proof you can go too.

05 Save & return

5 Things We Wish We Knew

Value-packed savable · voiceover · 45–60 sec

The save-and-return asset. Practical, specific and generous, it turns inspiration into a plannable trip, and the profile into a resource worth following.
